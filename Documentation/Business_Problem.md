# Business problem

Employee attrition creates operational and financial challenges for organizations. HR teams need to understand the size and distribution of employee exits and explore how attrition differs across workforce segments.

This project uses the IBM HR Analytics sample dataset to explore employee attrition patterns and related employee attributes.

## Objectives
- Quantify total employees, active employees, attrited employees, and attrition rate.
- Compare attrition across demographic and job-related categories.
- Explore overtime and salary-range patterns.
- Review the latest available satisfaction and performance ratings.
- Explore tenure, promotion timing, and current-role tenure among employees who left.

## Intended audience
HR managers, people analytics teams, and business stakeholders who need an interactive descriptive view of workforce attrition.

## Scope
This is descriptive analytics. It identifies patterns and associations in the available sample data; it does not establish causality or predict individual employee exits.
